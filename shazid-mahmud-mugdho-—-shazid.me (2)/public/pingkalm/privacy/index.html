Privacy Policy

PingKalm (“the app”, package com.pingkalm.app) is made by Shazid Mahmud. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, what stays on your device, and what Google AdMob may collect to show ads. By using PingKalm, you agree to this policy.

The short version: Your notifications are read, organised and stored on your device only — we never receive them, and we never sell them. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and diagnostics as described in Section 4.

1. No account, no sign-up

PingKalm requires no sign-up, login, or account creation. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password, and it does not create any account or profile for you.

2. Local-only notification processing

When you grant Notification Access, the app can read incoming notifications — the sending app, the sender, the message text and the time. All captured notifications, Focus Mode settings, and locally parsed MFS and bank transaction alerts (for example bKash, Nagad, Rocket and bank messages) are processed and stored strictly on your device, and used only to provide the app’s features:

  • Auto-cleaner: to identify and clear spam and promotional notifications from your status bar.
  • Private Vault & Unsent Chat Recovery: to keep a copy of messages that are later deleted or unsent, so you can still read them.
  • Cashflow ledger: to read MFS and bank alerts and sort them into a local cashflow ledger.
  • Focus Mode and Quiet Hours: to hold or silence notifications on the schedule you set.

Notification content is never uploaded, shared, sold, or transmitted to any external server. We do not operate servers that receive your notifications, and notification content is never used to build an advertising profile by us.

Android cloud backup is disabled for the app (allowBackup="false"), so your notification data and vault contents are not copied to any cloud backup. They exist only on your device.

3. Permissions we request

PingKalm requests the following permissions. Each one is used only for the purpose stated here, and you can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings — though some features will stop working if the permission they need is revoked.

  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService) — used solely to organise, filter, silence (Focus Mode), and locally parse notifications on your device. The app never sends notification content anywhere.
  • Do Not Disturb (DND) access — used only when you enable Focus Mode, to silence notifications while Focus Mode is on. When you turn Focus Mode off, or after your phone reboots, PingKalm restores your previous Do Not Disturb state.
  • Post Notifications — to show you PingKalm’s own alerts.
  • Run at startup — to restart the notification listener and restore Focus Mode after your phone reboots.
  • Ignore battery optimisations — so Android does not stop the listener in the background.
  • Biometric / fingerprint — optional, only if you choose to lock the app with your fingerprint or face.
  • Internet access — only to load ads from Google AdMob. Your notifications are not sent over the internet.

PingKalm respects Android 15+ sensitive notification and one-time password (OTP) protections: notifications that Android marks as sensitive, including OTP codes, are handled only as the system allows and are never extracted, uploaded or shared.

4. Advertising and third-party services

While your notification data stays completely on your device, PingKalm uses Google AdMob to display ads, and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage your ad consent choices.

AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your Advertising ID), IP address, and diagnostics to serve and measure ads. This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. You can change your ad consent choices at any time from the app’s settings, where the UMP consent form is available. We do not receive any personal information from AdMob about you.

5. Information sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which receives ad-related identifiers as described in Section 4. We will disclose information only if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because all of your data is stored locally on your device, we hold no copy of it and there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Your data remains on your phone until you delete it yourself — you can permanently delete all data at any time by clearing data inside the app or in Android Settings, or by uninstalling PingKalm.

7. Children’s privacy

PingKalm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove any locally stored data.

8. Security

Your data is protected by staying on your device, with Android cloud backup disabled (allowBackup="false"), and you can add an extra layer with the app’s optional biometric lock. No method of storage is completely secure, and keeping your phone’s lock screen and software up to date is the best protection for on-device data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the “Effective Date” at the top of this page will change, and the new version will apply from that date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or about PingKalm, including any privacy inquiry, write to us at hello@shazid.me.
